## Why the warnings are kept

- **Missing lead score**: the score is unknown, not zero. Imputing it would bias lead-quality averages, so these leads stay in volume KPIs and are excluded from score averages. The count goes *up* after cleaning because out-of-range scores (DQ12) are set to NULL rather than guessed.
- **Unknown campaign**: the lead is real and its channel is known; only the campaign tag is wrong. It is kept (campaign = NULL, shown as *Unassigned*) so channel totals stay complete.
